5. End-to-end flow

From website visit to pickup

How a single visit moves across the Website and the Staff Portal, start to finish.

Patient
Visits website, chats with AI or browses
Books appointment or submits patient intake form
Arrives for exam / fitting
Handoff to staff at the shop
Staff
Looks up patient & prior order history
Creates Rx or Non-Rx job order — status starts at Ordered
Takes deposit & prints job-order slip
Updates status to Ready once the order is prepared
Patient
Tracks the job order on the website using the order number & phone
Sees status: Being prepared / Ready for pick-up / Already claimed
Patient sees "Ready" and returns to collect order
Staff
Collects balance payment (Cash / Gcash / Check)
Marks the order Claimed
Payment posts to Orders & Sales reports same day
Closes the day — cash reconciliation locks the balances

Every job order moves through three statuses — Ordered → Ready → Claimed — visible live on the Staff Portal's Dashboard view, so any staff member can see where every order stands at a glance. The patient can check that same status on the website at any point, using just the order number and the last 4 digits of their phone — no login needed. Everything else — the order record, payments, balances, and reporting — runs through the Staff Portal, which is why an order paid down days after intake, or a payment taken on a different date than the order, still shows up correctly in the same day's reports and on the live Orders list.
