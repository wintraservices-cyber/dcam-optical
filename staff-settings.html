All AI features
Master switch — saves as soon as you flip it. When off, both the website chat and the staff assistant stop right away, whatever the settings below say, so there's no AI usage or cost.
Test mode Free
Saves as soon as you flip it. Both chats give sample answers instead of calling the AI, so there's no cost and no API key needed. The website chat uses your real Business info and stock; the staff assistant runs its real lookups using simple keywords and still follows the switches below. Chats show a "Test mode" label while it's on. It's also forced on if AI_TEST_MODE=1 is set in Vercel.

Website assistant

The "Ask us anything" chat on the public website. Always uses the details from the Business info tab.
Website chat on
When off, visitors see a short message pointing them to the intake form instead.
Share in-stock frames & lenses
Lets the website chat mention items in stock and their sale prices. Base (cost) prices are never shared.
Order status lookup
Customers can ask "are my glasses ready?" in the chat, or use its "Check my order" button. They must enter the job order number from their claim stub and the last 4 digits of the phone number on the order — orders are never looked up by name. The reply shows only whether it's being prepared, ready for pick-up or claimed, plus the due date. Repeated wrong tries are blocked.
Include remaining balance
Also tells the customer how much is left to pay (only after both numbers match).

Staff assistant

The chat bubble on staff pages. Choose exactly what it can look up, separately for admins and staff. It can only read data — it never creates, edits or deletes anything.
Staff assistant on
Turns the chat bubble on or off for everyone. Changes apply on the next message.
Can look upAdminStaff
To answer, the assistant sends only the records it looks up to the AI provider (Anthropic). API data isn't used to train their models. If you enable Patient records, mention AI-assisted processing in your privacy notice.
