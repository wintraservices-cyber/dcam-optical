What we do

Everything your eyes need, under one roof.

We built DCAM Optical to skip the parts of eye care that feel like a chore — long holds, confusing insurance calls, guessing at frames. What's left is just good care.

Comprehensive exams

Full vision and eye health screening, including retinal imaging, with a doctor who explains what they're actually looking at.

Contact lens fitting

First-time and returning wearers, including specialty fits for astigmatism, dry eye, and multifocal correction.

Frame styling

A curated collection with an optician who'll tell you the truth about what suits your face — not just what's in stock.

Prescription lenses

Single vision, progressives, blue-light, and transitions — ground and fit on-site by our lab partners in days, not weeks.

Pediatric eye care

Kid-friendly exams and a frame wall built for durability, because we know exactly where those glasses are headed.

Urgent eye concerns

Redness, irritation, sudden vision changes — same-week appointments held open for exactly this.
